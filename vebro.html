ABOUT VEBRO

Vebro Polymers is a global manufacturer of high-performance, seamless PU concrete and resin flooring systems, now available in phthalate-free (PF) formulations for healthier, safer industrial environments.

WHAT IS PU CONCRETE?

Polyurethane (PU) concrete is a seamless flooring system known for its extreme durability, chemical resistance, and hygienic properties. It is widely used in food processing plants, pharmaceuticals, and heavy industries.

PU vs EPOXY

Polyurethane (PU)

  • Better abrasion resistance
  • Better thermal shock resistance
  • Better flexibility (weight-bearing)
  • UV resistance
  • Less yellowing overtime
  • Matte finish

    • Epoxy

  • More brittle (cracking)
  • Less chemical resistance
  • Less thermal shock resistance
  • Yellowing from UV exposure
  • Glossy finish

    • PHTHALATES

    What are Phthalates?

    Phthalates are chemicals added to plastics (and some coatings) to increase flexibility, durability, etc.
